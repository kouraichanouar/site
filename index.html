I am a PhD student in mathematics at the Technical University of Munich, in the Mathematical Quantum Physics group of Prof. Simone Warzel.

My research interests lie in probability, analysis, and mathematical physics. In particular, I work on disordered systems, quantum statistical mechanics, and quantum information.

I am part of both SFB TRR 352: Mathematics of Many-Body Quantum Systems and the Munich Center for Quantum Science and Technology (MCQST).

Publications and preprints

Lower bound on the mixing time of p-spin glasses

Anouar Kouraich and Simone Warzel · 2026 · Preprint · arXiv:2605.14681

arXiv PDF Video (YouTube)
Overview

Studies the Glauber dynamics of classical p-spin glasses. The paper establishes exponential lower bounds on mixing times in a low-temperature regime by analyzing the energy landscape and its bottlenecks.

Gibbs State Postulate from Dynamical Stability

Vjosa Blakaj, Matthias C. Caro, Anouar Kouraich, Daniel Malz and Michael M. Wolf · Annales Henri Poincaré · Published online 11 November 2026

Journal / DOI arXiv PDF
Overview

Characterizes Gibbs states through dynamical stability, and shows that an additional assumption called the “zeroth law” is not needed. The result also identifies a class of oscillator environments sufficient for the characterization.

The Quantum Random Energy Model is the Limit of Quantum p-Spin Glasses

Anouar Kouraich, Chokri Manai and Simone Warzel · 2025 (arXiv) · Journal publication reported; citation details pending verification

arXiv PDF
Overview

Proves the convergence of the infinite-volume free energy of quantum p-spin glasses in a transverse magnetic field toward that of the quantum random energy model as p tends to infinity.
